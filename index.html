Shade № 01 · Pearl Base

About Me

Hi! I'm Ridhima.

I'm one of 50 students worldwide selected for the Jerome Fisher Program in Management & Technology (M&T) at the University of Pennsylvania, pursuing a dual degree: a B.S.E. in Mechanical Engineering at Penn Engineering and a B.S. in Economics with a finance concentration at Wharton. I'm also a Joseph Wharton Scholar.

The intersection of engineering, business, and art shows up everywhere I work: shipping AI agents on CoreWeave's finance team, teaching product strategy to fellows at Product Space, engineering hardware for Penn Hyperloop, and studying venture through Girls into VC.

Before Penn, I was salutatorian at Quarry Lane School in Dublin, California, captained a FIRST Robotics team, and co-founded a 501c3 with a patent-pending product. Off the clock, I love nail art, painting, fantasy writing, karate, and vlogging.
