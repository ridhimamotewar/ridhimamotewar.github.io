Shade № 01 · Pearl Base

About Me

RM

I'm Ridhima — an M&T student at Penn who believes software (and spreadsheets) should feel as considered as a hand-painted manicure.

I'm one of 50 students worldwide selected for the Jerome Fisher Program in Management & Technology at the University of Pennsylvania, pursuing a dual degree: a B.S.E. in Computer Science at Penn Engineering and a B.S. in Economics with a finance concentration at Wharton (Class of 2029). I'm also a Joseph Wharton Scholar.

Most recently I interned on CoreWeave's FP&A team, where I built three AI agents that saved analysts over two weeks of manual work. Before Penn, I was salutatorian at Quarry Lane School in Dublin, California, captained a FIRST Robotics team, and co-founded a 501c3 with a patent-pending product. Off the clock: nail art, painting, fantasy writing, karate, and vlogging.
